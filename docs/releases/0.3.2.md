Many of you already know his work from the Djules fork and his contributions to frame generation, SteamVR and Vulkan support. We will now be working together on the project's ongoing development. A big thank you to Djules for his work and for joining the project!

## What's new in 0.3.2

- **Star Citizen support:** OFXR's files are now digitally signed, so Easy Anti-Cheat lets OFXR load. Optical flow, Hybrid and DLSS-G work, with SteamVR and VDXR. Use at your own risk; see the README.
- **More Luke Ross games:** fixed Grand Theft Auto V Enhanced support and added support for Cyberpunk 2077, Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora, The Last of Us Part I, and Uncharted: Legacy of Thieves Collection (Uncharted 4 and The Lost Legacy).
- **New OSR option for Luke Ross:** OFXR Stereo Reconstruction replaces AFW as the default, with less ghosting and better performance. AFW remains available as **AFW (legacy)**.
- **Improved performance** with Luke Ross OSR and AFW.
- **Motion-vector support for Ready or Not.**
- **Motion-vector support for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 with foveated rendering.**
- **Compatibility with Cheeky Foveated DLSS**, except in Luke Ross games, which still need a corresponding fix on the Cheeky Foveated DLSS side.
- **Motion-vector support for Vulkan games.**
- **AutoFPS support** through the optional `status.json` export.
- **Lower VRAM usage.**
- **Fixed Neural Rendering when using a reduced working resolution.**
- **OpenKneeboard support**, including interpolation of compatible OpenXR panel images and poses.

**Upgrading from 0.3.1:** close the old tray and the game first. "AFW Only" is now **Frame Reconstruction only**, and Luke Ross profiles start on OSR. Select **AFW (legacy)** to go back. In the REAL VR menu, use Legacy AER and turn off Camera rotation compensation.

Please read the [README](https://github.com/tig3rmast3r/OFXR-Bridge#readme) for setup instructions, requirements and more information.
